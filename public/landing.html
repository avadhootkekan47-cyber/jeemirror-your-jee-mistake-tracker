Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make the payment?

Payments are processed manually via UPI to keep costs absolutely zero for hosting. You simply scan the QR code (UPI ID: 9370939333@ybl), pay Rs. 50 or 350, and submit your UTR number. Your account is activated within 2 hours.

Is my test data and personal info safe?

Yes. The app is built on a production-grade PostgreSQL database via Supabase. We utilize strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies, meaning it is cryptographically impossible for anyone (even us) to access your mistake data except your logged-in account.

Can I use this for NEET or other exams?

While the current subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Maths) are hardcoded for JEE, the core concept of tracking mistakes applies to any exam. A customizable subjects feature is planned for the future!
